Oral Defense Demonstration Guide:
  • Click "Validate & Submit" on empty form: All fields turn red (.is-invalid) displaying respective .invalid-feedback notices.
  • Click "Fill Valid": Populates compliant data and turns every field green (.is-valid) with "Looks good!" messages.
